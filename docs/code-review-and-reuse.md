## What reviewers are seeing

The five C# examples are *independently authored portfolio models*. They are complete, inspectable samples of selected engineering contracts but **not extracted production Unity files**. Their executable checks validate their own simplified contract only, **not** a production build, ML-Agents training, frame-rate performance, networking consistency, or device behavior.

Production code, Unity scenes, imported assets, prefabs, materials, game builds, internal logs, secret credentials, private Git history, and proprietary third-party raw assets are **not included** in the intended release.

Game-engine behavior described in the case studies is based on a reviewed source revision; any future video must accurately label its build, observed outcomes, and limitations.
